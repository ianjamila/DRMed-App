Chapter 1

How a visit flows

One patient, one trip to the clinic, eight hand-offs. Every chapter in this guide is one of these boxes in more detail. Reading this page first makes everything else obvious.

Doctor consultations follow the same path but skip the lab: after the patient has seen the doctor, Admin or the pathologist marks the consultation done, and a consult-only visit prints no receipt.

Two gates you will bump into

The system enforces two rules in the database itself. No button, role or password gets around them, so it is worth knowing them by heart.

Lab gate
A test does not appear on the lab bench until the visit is paid, waived, or billed to an HMO. Until then it sits in the reception queue under Waiting for payment, and the lab sees an awaiting payment chip on the results archive.
Release gate
A finished result cannot be released to the patient until the visit is paid or waived. There is no HMO exception here: an HMO-billed visit passes the lab gate but stays unpaid until Admin records the HMO settlement or waives the balance. See Known limitations.

The life of a test

Every test line on a visit moves along this ladder. The words are the ones you will see in status chips.

requested→ in progress→ ready for release→ released
requested
Reception has billed it. Nobody has picked it up yet.
in progress
A medtech or x-ray tech has claimed it and is working on it.
ready for release
Finalised. Reception can release it once the visit is paid.
released
The patient can see and download it in the portal.
cancelled / deleted
Removed from the queue by reception before payment. Nothing is billed for it, and it can be restored.
Also

A status called result uploaded exists for tests that require a pathologist's sign-off before release. No service uses it today and the sign-off screen is not yet built. Leave Requires sign-off off on every service until it is. See Known limitations.

The life of a visit's money

unpaid
Nothing recorded yet. The visit can still be deleted from the queue.
partial
Some payment recorded, balance outstanding.
paid
Fully settled. Lab and release both open.
waived
Admin decided not to collect. Treated as paid. Cannot be deleted.

Payments are never edited or deleted. A wrong payment is voided (with a reason) and a fresh one recorded. Every void, every release, every print and every download is written to the audit log, because the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) requires it.

Three identifiers

DRM-0001
The patient's permanent DRM-ID. Assigned once, printed on every receipt, used to sign in to the portal.
Visit #
A running number per visit. Patients quote it when they call. Search it with or without leading zeros.
Secure PIN
Eight characters, issued per visit, valid 60 days, shown on the printed receipt only until it is printed. Only the newest PIN works: a new visit issues a new PIN which replaces the old one.

2.1 Signing in

  1. Open drmed.ph/staff/login.

    Patients use a different door, drmed.ph/portal. Don't mix them up when helping someone at the counter.

  2. Enter your work email and password, then Sign in.

    You land on the dashboard for your role.

No reset link

There is no self-service Forgot password for staff. Ask an Admin to reset it under Staff users. Too many wrong attempts in a row lock sign-in for a few minutes.

2.2 Two-step sign-in (MFA)

A 6-digit code from an authenticator app on top of your password. Required for Admins. Optional but recommended for everyone else, and once you have set it up it is always asked for.

  1. After signing in, go to /staff/mfa.

  2. Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password or similar.

  3. Type the 6-digit code the app shows and verify.

Lost phone

Tell an Admin immediately. They remove your factor so you can sign in and enrol the new phone. There are no recovery codes.

Chapter 3

Reception

ReceptionAdminWhere: the Front desk, Billing and Services groups of the sidebar

Reception is the front door and the cashier. Almost every patient touches your screens twice: on arrival, to register and pay, and again when they come back for their result. The centre of your day is the Reception Queue; the screen you will use most carefully is New visit. Results themselves are released by the lab team, not by reception (3.8).

3.1 Before the patient arrives

Patients can book and even register themselves online. Nothing requires them to. A pure walk-in with no online step must always work, and it does.

Appointments

Sidebar › Front desk › Appointments · /staff/appointments
Tabs
All · Consultations · Home service
Sections
Pending callback · Today · Next 30 days
Row actions
Mark arrived · No-show · Cancel · ↶ Revert · + Start visit

Every booking made on the public /schedule page lands here, one row per service. Bookings for a doctor who has no published schedule, and all home-service requests, arrive as pending callback: the patient has been told you will call within one working day to agree a time.

  1. Work the Pending callback section first thing.

    Call the patient, agree the time, and confirm the booking.

  2. When a booked patient walks in, click Mark arrived on their row.

    Only a confirmed booking can be marked arrived. Grouped multi-service bookings show one button for all of them.

  3. Click + Start visit.

    It opens New visit with the patient already picked. The button appears only after arrived and only if the booking has a patient record. A walk-in booking taken by phone has no record yet, so register the patient first (3.2) and start the visit from their page.

Taking a booking by phone

  1. Click + New appointment in the page header.

    A panel slides in from the right. Its note says it: for a walk-in who is ready now, create the visit instead.

  2. Choose the patient mode: Existing, New or Walk-in.

    • Existing: search by name, phone or DRM-ID. Their upcoming bookings show so you don't double-book.
    • New: a short form. Email is needed so the system can match duplicates and send the confirmation.
    • Walk-in: just a name and phone. No patient record is created; you register them when they arrive.

  3. Pick the booking type and services.

    Diagnostic package, Lab request, Doctor appointment or Home service. Doctor appointments need a physician and a slot; packages and most lab tests need no time.

  4. If an amber ⚠ Scheduling conflict box appears, read it, then either change the slot or click Book anyway.

    You may override closures, doctor hours and taken slots. The override is recorded with your name.

  5. Tick Send confirmation (SMS + email) if the patient wants one, then Create.

In practice

Confirmations go out by email. The SMS channel exists in the software but has never been switched on, so tell patients to watch their inbox, not their texts.

Let patients register themselves

The Registration link button in the Appointments header shows a QR code that opens the public /register form. Copy link to send it by chat, or Open printable poster → for a counter poster. Patients who register or book online arrive in your Patients list flagged Pre-registered · verify. Check their ID at the counter (3.3).

3.2 Register a new patient

Sidebar › Front desk › New patient registration · /staff/patients/new
Page says

Required

Ends with

  1. Search first. Type the surname or phone into Patients before creating anyone.

    Seven thousand patients already exist. Duplicates cost admin a merge later.

  2. Fill in Identity: First name, Last name, Middle name (optional), Birthdate, Sex, Phone, Email.

    Birthdate is typed as YYYY-MM-DD. Email is optional but must be a real address if given.

    As you type, the form may show Possible existing patient(s): with an exact match badge and a Use this patient shortcut. Use it. If you insist on creating a second record for an exact match, the system asks you to confirm.

  3. Fill in Marketing & preferences.

    Referral source (and Referred by doctor when relevant) and Preferred result release: Physical pickup at clinic, Email, Viber, GCash or Pickup at counter. The release dialog later defaults to this choice.

  4. Record the Senior / PWD ID if they have one: ID kind (Senior Citizen or PWD) and ID number.

    This is captured once so you never re-ask. The 20% statutory discount on a visit prints the ID number on the receipt from here.

  5. Have them sign the printed registration & consent form, then tick Data privacy consent (RA 10173) and choose who signed: Patient, Guardian or Representative.

    The form to print is on the patient's page (Print form). Consent is recorded once and stays on file.

  6. Click Create patient.

    The patient's DRM-ID is assigned (for example DRM-7052) and you land on their page. Click + Start visit to continue.

Why consent matters

Admin can switch on a consent gate that blocks result release for any patient without consent on file. It is off today, and only a handful of existing patients have consent recorded, so capture it at every registration and every first visit of a returning patient. It takes one tick.

3.4 Create the visit

Sidebar › Services › New lab request / New imaging request, or + Start visit on a patient · /staff/visits/new
Tabs
Doctor · Lab & Services (with sub-chips All / Lab / Imaging)
Ends with
Create visit & issue PIN
Not here
Payment. You take it on the next screen.

A visit is one trip to the clinic. It bundles every test, package, consultation or procedure the patient is having today. If you open the screen without a patient it first asks you to pick one: search, click Start visit →, or + New patient to register.

  1. Confirm the Patient card at the top is the right person.

  2. Under Select services, tick each test, package, consult or procedure.

    • Use the Lab & Services tab for tests, imaging and packages; Doctor for consultations and procedures. Each tab shows a count of what you have ticked.
    • A package row lists what it Includes. Tick the package, not its parts.
    • There is no quantity box. A service is on the visit or it isn't.

  3. Apply Line discounts where they apply.

    Each line has a dropdown starting at No discount. The choices are whatever Admin set up under Discounts, including the statutory Senior / PWD 20%. A senior discount on a service that is not eligible for it is simply ignored, so you cannot over-discount by mistake.

  4. For an HMO patient, fill in the HMO block.

    There are two, because lab work and doctor fees are often approved separately: Lab & Services HMO and Doctor / PF HMO. Pick the Provider (the default is — Cash / no HMO —), then enter the Approval date and Authorization no. from the LOA. An HMO visit reaches the lab without a cash payment.

  5. For a consultation or procedure, pick the Attending physician and check the fee.

    • Consultation fee (₱) pre-fills from the doctor's default. It must be above zero.
    • A procedure pre-fills its catalog price; you may type a Procedure description and an HMO approved (₱) amount.
    • The physician is required whenever the doctor earns a professional fee (PF) from the line. If you skip it the release will be blocked later, so pick it now.

  6. Click Create visit & issue PIN.

    The system assigns a visit number, creates one line per service, and issues a 60-day Secure PIN. You are taken to the receipt.

Doctor + lab on the same day

If you tick lines on both tabs, the system creates two visits that share one receipt: one for the doctor, one for the lab. They are folded together in the archive and the queue, but each has its own visit number and payment status. This keeps the doctor's fee separate from lab revenue.

Consult-only visits

A visit with only a consultation prints no receipt. You land on the visit page with a note explaining why. If the patient will need portal access (for a later lab visit on the same account they get a new PIN anyway), use Issue portal PIN on the visit page, which prints a portal-access slip with no charges on it.

3.5 The receipt and the Secure PIN

/staff/visits/[visit]/receipt · also Receipt on any visit page and Print billing in the queue
Shows
Clinic header · patient and DRM-ID · consent on file or not · every line and discount · totals · the Patient Portal Access box
Portal box
DRM-ID · Secure PIN · Sign in at drmed.ph/portal to view results when ready. PIN is valid for 60 days.

  1. Print it straight away and hand it to the patient.

    The PIN stays on screen only until you click Print, or for five minutes, whichever comes first. After that the box reads Already viewed — re-issue from admin if needed. Nobody, including Admin, can look it up later.

  2. Point to the portal box and say the two things that matter.

    Your results will be at drmed.ph/portal. Sign in with this DRM-ID and this PIN. The PIN works for 60 days.

  3. If they lose it, they come back with an ID and you re-issue.

    Open the patient's page, click Re-issue PIN. A new PIN for their latest visit replaces the old one and prints on a portal-access slip. See 3.9.

Never

Never write the PIN anywhere else, read it over the phone to a caller you cannot identify, or send it by chat. Anyone holding the DRM-ID and PIN can read that patient's results.

3.6 Take payment

Record payment on the visit page or in the Reception Queue · /staff/payments/new
Shows

Fields

Ends with

  1. From the visit page (or the queue's Waiting for payment tab) click Record payment.

  2. Choose the method. Enter the amount received and, for e-wallet, card or bank, the reference number.

    You may record less than the balance. The visit shows partial until the rest is paid.

  3. Click Record payment.

    The visit flips to paid the moment the balance reaches zero. The lab bench sees the tests immediately; nothing else needs clicking.

HMO visits

An HMO-billed visit skips the lab gate, so the tests run without a cash payment. The patient still pays any co-pay or non-covered lines now. The visit itself stays unpaid until Admin records the HMO settlement under HMO claims or waives the balance, and until then the lab cannot release the result. Agree the standing rule for HMO patients with Admin before go-live; see Known limitations.

Undoing a payment

  1. Open the visit, find the payment under Payments, click Void.

  2. Type the reason and confirm.

    The dialog reminds you that a reversal is booked automatically. The payment stays visible under Voided payments, the balance re-opens, and a gift code used for it becomes usable again.

  3. Record the correct payment.

    A void is never un-voided.

Waiving a balance (deciding not to collect) is Admin-only: Waive balance → Confirm waive on the visit page. A waived visit releases like a paid one but can no longer be deleted.

3.7 The Reception Queue

Sidebar › Front desk › Reception Queue · /staff/visits/queue
Stages
Waiting for payment · Processing · Completed
Row actions
Record payment · Open visit · Print billing

This is today's worklist. It updates on its own as payments land and tests finish.

Waiting for payment
Visits created but not yet paid. The lab cannot see these. Collect and Record payment.
Processing
Paid or waived, and the lab or imaging still owes at least one result. The row itself lists what is outstanding, which is all you need to answer is it ready?
Completed
Paid and every lab and imaging line released. Print billing reprints the receipt (without the PIN).
Why the visit page shows no tests

By clinic policy, reception does not see individual test lines or result PDFs. On a visit page you see the patient, the payments, the receipt and PIN buttons, the attending physician and the HMO details, but not the tests. Use the Reception Queue row for progress, and leave releasing to the lab.

The related Visits item in the Billing group has three tabs: Reception Queue, New visit and Archive. The archive filters by Lab Tests / Doctor Consults / Doctor Procedures, shows Active or Deleted entries, and searches by visit number. Admin can Export CSV from it.

3.8 When the result is ready

Releasing a result (making it visible to the patient, and emailing them if they asked) is done by the medtech or x-ray tech who finished it, or by Admin, from the visit page. Reception's part is everything around it.

  1. Watch the Reception Queue. A visit moves from Processing to Completed when the last result is released.

    If a patient asks by phone, search the visit number in the queue or the Visits archive and read the row.

  2. If the patient wants a printed copy at the counter, ask the lab to print it when they release.

    Reception cannot open result PDFs. The lab chooses Physical or Pickup as the release method, prints the PDF and hands it to you.

  3. If the patient wants it by email, nothing more is needed.

    The lab releases with Email and the patient receives Your DRMed lab result is ready with the portal link. They sign in with the DRM-ID and PIN from their receipt.

  4. If the lab says a release is blocked, fix the cause on your side.

    • Visit must be paid before results can be released: record the payment, or ask Admin to waive.
    • Patient data-privacy consent is not on file: print and capture consent on the patient's page (3.3).
    • An attending physician is required…: click Assign physician in the Attending physician section of the visit. The page warns you in amber beforehand.

  5. For a consultation or procedure, tell Admin (or the pathologist) once the patient has seen the doctor.

    They click Mark consultation done or Mark procedure done on the visit. That is what accrues the doctor's professional fee. Reception cannot see doctor lines on the visit page.

Dashboard card

The Pending release card on the reception dashboard opens the lab queue, which is always empty for reception. Use the Reception Queue's Processing tab instead.

3.9 Fix-ups on the visit page

Wrong test billed, not yet paid
Delete the visit and give a reason, then create it again correctly. Nothing is billed for a deleted visit; it moves to Deleted entries and to Admin's Deleted queue entries report, and Restore brings it back. Removing a single line instead of the whole visit needs Admin, who can see the lines. Once any payment is recorded, deletion is no longer possible: void the payment first.
Patient lost the receipt / PIN
Open the patient's page, Re-issue PIN. A fresh 60-day PIN for their latest visit prints on a Patient portal access slip. The old PIN stops working. (The visit page's Issue portal PIN appears only on consult-only visits, which never had a receipt.)
Forgot the doctor
Attending physician section → Assign physician (or Change).
Result needs correcting after release
Tell the lab. They undo the release, amend with a reason, and release again (4.7). If the patient had already opened it, phone them.
Wrong patient entirely
If unpaid, delete the visit and create it again under the right patient. If paid, void the payment first.

Chapter 4

Lab & imaging

MedtechX-ray technicianPathologistAdminWhere: the Lab group of the sidebar

The lab team owns the queue: everything reception has billed and the patient has paid for, waiting to be done. You claim a test, enter the values or attach the report, finalise, and then release it to the patient. You never touch money and you never need the patient's DRM-ID; the bench works by name.

4.3 Enter a structured result

A single test · /staff/queue/[test]
Heading
Service code, name and status chip; patient name, age and sex
Form
Enter result values: one row per parameter, a Blank box on every row
Buttons
Save draft · Finalise & generate PDF

Most tests have a structured template. The form is built from it: numbers, drop-downs or free text, with the unit and the reference range beside each field. Ranges are chosen for this patient's age and sex automatically, and rows that don't apply to their sex are hidden.

  1. Type each value.

    • A value outside the reference range turns the box red. That is the H / L flag which prints on the PDF. A drop-down answer listed as abnormal turns red the same way (flag A).
    • Dual-unit tests (two columns, SI and Conv.) convert automatically when you fill either side. Don't type both.
    • Didn't run a sub-test? Tick Blank on that row. It prints as a dash. Leaving it empty instead stops you finalising.

  2. Click Save draft whenever you step away.

    Drafts keep everything you typed. The test stays in progress.

  3. When every row is filled or blanked, click Finalise & generate PDF.

    If anything is missing you get Missing values for: …. Mark blank if you didn't run the sub-test. On success: ✓ Saved. Control No. … — finalised. The test becomes ready for release and the PDF is generated with your name on it.

Finalise means finished

After finalising, the only way to change a value is an amendment with a written reason (4.7), and the original stays on record. Check the row against the analyser printout before you click.

Critical values

A numeric value beyond a critical threshold is recorded as a critical alert the moment you finalise. You will not see a pop-up; the pathologist and admin see it on their Critical alerts page (4.8). Tell them in person as well. That is a clinical duty, not a software one.

4.4 The chemistry panel (one form for the whole order)

Chemistry tests (FBS, lipid profile, creatinine, and so on) are reported together on one consolidated form so the patient gets one chemistry report, not six. Clicking any chemistry test in the queue lands you here.

  1. Click Claim this report.

    Until you claim, the value fields are hidden. If a colleague has it you see This report is claimed by another medtech.

Every field greyed out?

The screen will say so in plain words: it is a template configuration problem, not a data-entry one. Report it to Admin (the group template under Result templates has no fields mapped for the ordered tests). Don't work around it by typing into another test.

4.5 Upload a PDF result (send-outs and tests without a template)

Some tests come back as a report from a partner lab, and a few have no structured template yet. For these the test page shows an upload form instead: Send-out test: attach the partner-lab PDF. or No structured template configured for this service yet — falling back to PDF upload.

  2. Choose the PDF result file. PDF only, up to 10 MB.

  3. Add Internal notes (optional) if there is something reception should know. Notes are staff-only; the patient never sees them.

  4. Click Upload result.

    The test moves to ready for release.

4.6 Imaging reports (X-ray, ultrasound, ECG)

Imaging services use the Imaging report template: each finding is a free-text box with its own Blank tick, plus a required Attached image.

  1. Claim the study from the Imaging queue and type the findings and impression.

  2. Attach the image or the radiologist's report: JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF, up to 25 MB.

    An image is embedded in the released PDF; a PDF is listed as a separate attachment. HEIC photos from an iPhone are not accepted; export as JPEG first.

  3. Click Finalise & generate PDF.

    Finalising without an attachment is refused: Please attach an image before finalising.

Releasing

Admin

AdminWhere: the Admin group of the sidebar, in six collapsible sub-groups

Admin can do everything reception and the lab can, and owns the settings behind them: the catalog and prices, the doctors and what they are paid, staff logins, the audit trail, and the books. This chapter is ordered the way a new clinic sets itself up, then the way a running clinic checks itself each day.

5.1 Setting up the clinic (in this order)

Each step depends on the one before it. All items are under Admin › Catalog & setup unless noted.

  1. Staff users Admin tools

    + New staff user: Email, Full name, Role (Reception · Medical Tech · X-ray Technician · Pathologist · Admin), Initial password (10 characters or more). After creating, edit the user to tick Active and record their PRC license (RMT, MD, RT or Pathologist, with number). Medtech licence numbers print on result PDFs.

  2. Enrol your own two-step sign-in.

    Admins are sent to /staff/mfa automatically. Other roles must type that address themselves; there is no link to it yet.

  3. Physicians

    + New physician: Full name, Specialty, Group, then the money: Compensation arrangement (PF split (standard), Rent-paying (keeps full consult) or Shareholder (keeps full consult)), Default consultation fee (₱) and Clinic cut (₱). A blank clinic cut means ₱100 for PF-split doctors and ₱0 for the other two. Then edit the physician to add a photo and open Schedule: add Recurring blocks (day, start, end, valid from/until) and one-off Overrides (a date off, or changed hours). A doctor with no recurring blocks is by appointment: online bookings for them become pending callbacks.

  4. Services

    + New service: Code, Name, Description, DRMed price (PHP), HMO price (PHP, optional), tick Senior / PWD 20% discount applies where the law applies, Turnaround (hours), Kind (Lab test · Lab package · Doctor consultation · Doctor procedure · Home service · Vaccine), Section, and Send-out test with the partner lab and unit cost when the test leaves the building. Active makes it visible on the website. Changing a price asks you to Confirm price change and keeps the history.

  5. Prices

    A spreadsheet-style view of every service's DRMed (₱) and HMO (₱) price for bulk updates. Consultations show priced at counter because reception types the fee per visit.

  6. Discounts

    The list reception sees on every visit line. The Senior / PWD row is Statutory, fixed at 20% by law and cannot be edited or switched off. + New discount for promos: Label, Code, Percent off or Fixed peso amount, Active, Order. Retire a promo by unticking Active; never rely on deleting.

  7. HMO providers

    + New provider: Provider name, Active, Days to pay invoice, contract dates, primary contact. This list feeds the two HMO drop-downs on New visit and the claims aging.

  8. Result templates

    The index groups services into Report groups (consolidated templates), With template, Eligible without template and Send-out (templates not applicable), and warns about templates with zero parameters or no mapped services. Edit a template's Layout (Simple · Dual unit · Multi-section · Imaging report), header and footer notes, and its parameters (name, input type, units, reference ranges by sex and age band, allowed and abnormal values, critical thresholds). Use Preview PDF to see the printed result before it goes live. The chemistry panel is one group template; the per-service chemistry templates are switched off on purpose and changing them does nothing.

  9. Closures, consent gate, dashboard cards

    • Closures (Operations): add a date and reason; the public booking page hides that day. If bookings already exist, Move to callback first.
    • Consent gate (Admin tools): Require consent before result release. Turn it on only after reception is capturing consent at every visit; today only a handful of patients have consent on file, so switching it on cold would block most releases.
    • Dashboard settings: per role, tick which cards show. Cards marked Sensitive reveal money.

  10. Gift codes and inventory (Operations)

    Gift codes › + Generate batch (how many, face value, label) creates codes for reception to sell; the Sales report shows who bought what. Inventory › + New item then record movements (Receive · Issue · Adjust · Expire · Count); the list flags Low / out and Expiring ≤ 60d. Medtechs and x-ray techs can see and post inventory too.

6.1 Viewing your results online

  1. Go to drmed.ph/portal on any phone or computer.

  2. Type your DRM-ID (it looks like DRM-0001) and the 8-character Secure PIN printed on your receipt, then tap Access my results.

  5. Tap Sign out when you are done, especially on a shared device.

If something goes wrong

Invalid DRM-ID or PIN.

The download won't open

No results yet

6.2 Booking and registering online

Both are optional. Walking in always works.

Book at drmed.ph/schedule

  1. Answer Have you visited DRMed before?

    No, I'm new registers you as part of the booking. Yes, I have a DRM-ID asks for the DRM-ID and last name so you skip retyping details.

  2. Choose what you are booking.

    • Diagnostic Package: no time slot. Just walk in during clinic hours.
    • Laboratory Request: pick the tests; only ultrasound needs a time. You can upload the doctor's request form.
    • Doctor Appointment: pick a specialty, a doctor and a slot. Some doctors are by appointment; the clinic calls you to confirm.
    • Home Service: the clinic calls to confirm a time and the fee.

  3. Fill in your details (new patients), review, tick the service agreement, and tap Confirm booking or Submit request.

    You see Booking confirmed. or Request received. plus Your DRM-ID if you are new. An email confirmation follows, with a link to cancel. A reminder email arrives the day before.

Signed-in portal users can also book from the Book link in the portal header without re-entering anything.

Register ahead at drmed.ph/register

Scan the QR poster at the counter or open the link. Enter name, birthdate, sex, phone and email, tick the privacy consent, tap Register. You get your DRM-ID on screen and by email. If you were already in the system the page says We found your record. and emails the DRM-ID instead of showing it. Reception checks your ID on arrival either way.

6.3 Privacy consent (RA 10173)

The clinic asks for your consent once, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, before processing your health information. You can give it three ways: by signing the printed form at reception, by ticking the box when you register online, or by accepting the notice the first time you sign in to the portal. You can withdraw it at any time by telling reception. Every view and download of your results is logged, and the clinic never sends results by text message.

Chapter 8

Known limitations (September 2026)

Things the screens hint at but don't do yet, so nobody wastes time looking for them.

HMO patients and release
An HMO-billed visit stays unpaid until the HMO settles, which can take months, and the release gate has no HMO exception. Until the clinic decides a rule (Admin waives HMO visits on the day, or the gate is changed), HMO patients cannot receive results online. Raise this with Admin before go-live.
Reception and results
Reception sees no test lines or PDFs on a visit (clinic policy since May 2026). Releasing and printing results is the lab's job. The reception dashboard's Pending release card opens the lab queue, which is empty for reception.
Doctor lines
Consultation and procedure lines are visible only to Admin and pathologists, so only they can click Mark consultation done. Reception can still assign the physician and record the fee.
Appointments after arrival
An appointment stays at arrived after the visit is created; nothing marks it completed. The Walk-ins waiting dashboard count therefore grows over time.
Pathologist sign-off
The Sign-off page is a placeholder and the Requires pathologist sign-off setting on services is disabled. Results go from the bench straight to ready for release. Pathologists review via Critical alerts and amendments instead.
SMS
No text messages are sent to patients. Booking confirmations, reminders and result-ready notices are email only. Patients with no email get no automatic notice.
MFA for non-admins
Nothing links to the enrolment page; type /staff/mfa. Once enrolled it is enforced.
Self-service unclaim
Only Admin can unclaim or reassign a test.
Doctor procedures
The catalog has no Doctor procedure services yet, so that path is untested in daily use. Add them under Services before billing procedures.
Report exports
The seven pages under Books & reports have no CSV export; the Daily report tabs do.
Marketing trackers
Stored in the browser that entered them. They don't sync between computers.
Parked pages
Sell gift code, Registration link, Sign-off and Patient receivables are under Hidden tabs (Admin only in the sidebar). Reception can still open Sell gift code and Registration link by typing the address.
Chapter 9

Glossary

Visit
One trip to the clinic. Bundles every test, consult or procedure done that day. Has a visit number and a payment status. A doctor-plus-lab day is two visits on one receipt.
Visit #
The running number per visit, printed on the receipt. Search it with or without leading zeros.
DRM-ID
The patient's permanent ID, e.g. DRM-0001.
Secure PIN
8 characters, issued per visit, valid 60 days, printed once. With the DRM-ID it opens the portal.
Pre-registered
A patient who registered or booked online and hasn't shown an ID at the counter yet.
Lab gate
The rule that hides unpaid, non-HMO visits from the lab bench.
Release
Making a finished result visible to the patient. Needs a paid or waived visit.
Waived
Admin decided not to collect the balance. Counts as paid.
Void
Cancelling a recorded payment with a reason. The record stays, the balance re-opens.
Package
A bundle of tests sold at one price. On a visit it is a ₱0 header line with the tests underneath.
Report group
Several tests reported on one form and one PDF: the chemistry panel.
Send-out
A test run by a partner lab. The medtech uploads the partner's PDF.
HMO
The patient's health plan. An HMO visit reaches the lab unpaid; release still needs the visit marked paid or waived.
PF
Professional fee: the doctor's share of a consultation or procedure. Accrues when reception marks the line done; Admin pays it out.
Clinic cut
The clinic's share of a consultation fee for PF-split doctors.
Attending physician
The doctor recorded on a visit. Required whenever a line earns PF.
Critical alert
An automatic flag when a finalised value crosses a critical threshold. Acknowledged by a pathologist or admin.
Amendment
A correction to a finalised result, with a reason. The original is kept.
EOD
End of day: counting the drawer by denomination and closing the shift.
Audit log
The tamper-evident record of every action, view, print and download. Required by RA 10173.
Consent gate
An admin switch that blocks release for patients without consent on file. Off today.
